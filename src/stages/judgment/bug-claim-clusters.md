## Defects already on the BugClaim path

Verifiers are checking these defects, reported by finders as bug claims. A
candidate below that reports the same underlying defect as one of them — same
root cause, even when worded as a judgment call — is a drop with the reason
"BugClaim-path claim: duplicates [cN]". A candidate about a different concern
on the same lines is judged on its own merits.
